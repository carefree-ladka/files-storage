# NovaDesk Product FAQ

## Product Overview

NovaDesk is a fictional customer support platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses.

It allows support teams to create tickets, assign owners, track priorities, and generate reports.

## Ticket Management

Every ticket has a unique ticket ID.

Each ticket has one primary owner.

Additional team members can follow a ticket to receive updates.

New tickets have Normal priority by default unless an automation rule specifies a different priority.

## Ticket Retention

Deleted tickets are soft-deleted initially.

Soft-deleted tickets remain recoverable for 30 days.

After 30 days, the cleanup process permanently removes them.

## Reporting

Workspace administrators can export ticket reports as CSV files.

Standard users can view reports but cannot export them.

Reports can include ticket IDs, categories, priorities, owners, statuses, and creation dates.

## Notifications

Urgent tickets trigger in-app notifications for the on-call group.

Email notifications depend on the notification settings configured for the workspace.

If notifications are not received, administrators should first verify the workspace notification settings.

## Internet Connectivity

NovaDesk requires an internet connection to create, update, and synchronize tickets.

Offline ticket creation is not supported.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a ticket have multiple primary owners?

A: No. Every ticket has exactly one primary owner.

Q: Can standard users export CSV reports?

A: No. Only workspace administrators can export reports.

Q: How long are deleted tickets retained?

A: For 30 days before permanent deletion.

Q: Does NovaDesk support offline mode?

A: No. An internet connection is required.

Q: What is the default ticket priority?

A: Normal priority, unless an automation rule overrides it.
