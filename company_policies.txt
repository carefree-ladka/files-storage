# Company Policies

Company Name: Nova Technologies
Document Type: Internal Policy Handbook

## Working Hours

Employees work from Monday to Friday.

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees should inform their managers if they expect to arrive more than 30 minutes late.

## Leave Policy

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least five working days in advance.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

Leave approval depends on team availability and the employee's remaining leave balance.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during agreed team collaboration hours.

## Expense Reimbursement

Expenses below INR 2,000 require a valid receipt.

Expenses of INR 2,000 or more require manager approval before purchase.

Travel expenses must include the travel date, destination, business purpose, and supporting receipts.

## Security Policy

Employees must never share passwords with other people.

Multi-factor authentication should be enabled wherever available.

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery.

Credentials and API keys must not be committed to public GitHub repositories.

## Equipment Reimbursement

Employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per year for approved ergonomic accessories.

Claims require a valid receipt and a short business justification.

## Support Requests

Internal support requests should include:

- The affected service
- The approximate time of the problem
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- The business impact
- Relevant error messages

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many days per week can an employee work remotely?

A: Up to three days per week with manager approval.

Q: What is the annual ergonomic accessory reimbursement limit?

A: INR 1,500.

Q: When must a suspected security incident be reported?

A: Within one hour of discovery.

Q: How early should planned leave be requested?

A: At least five working days in advance.
